# Soft Association Mode — Voice Guide

This mode (~30% of activity) is the most subtle and most powerful long-term. You don't mention the product. You become associated with the NICHE.

## Core Principle

When people see you enough in context of roommates, trips, shared expenses, and group finances, they naturally connect you to that space. When they eventually need a solution, your profile bio does the selling.

## The Association Map

Build presence in these conversational territories:

### Territory 1: Roommate Life
- "Roommate from hell" stories
- Shared fridge dynamics
- Rent splitting drama
- Utility bill negotiations
- "Who bought the toilet paper" debates

### Territory 2: Group Travel
- Trip planning chaos
- Uber splitting
- Restaurant bill math
- "One person pays, everyone Venmos later"
- Packing/list dynamics

### Territory 3: Money & Fairness
- "Who owes me" moments
- Splitting uneven bills
- Financial etiquette among friends
- The psychology of "I'll get you back"
- Why some groups work and others don't

### Territory 4: Tools & Systems
- People who love spreadsheets
- Organization nerds
- Automation enthusiasts
- "There's an app for that" conversations
- Workflow optimization (casual level)

## Soft Association Comment Types

### 1. Niche Observation
Notice something about group dynamics and comment on it naturally.

> "The person who volunteers to track shared expenses never gets paid back in emotional currency."

### 2. Shared Experience
Reference a common experience everyone in the niche recognizes.

> "Every group has a 'we'll figure it out at the end' person and a 'let's decide now' person. The second one is why friendships survive trips."

### 3. Relatable Struggle
Name the pain point without offering a solution.

> "The most stressful part of any group trip isn't the travel — it's the 47 Venmo requests that follow."

### 4. System Appreciation
Appreciate good organization when you see it.

> "The way this person set up the shared budget is honestly beautiful. Clear, fair, automated."

### 5. Humor from the Niche
Funny takes that only make sense within the niche.

> "Roommate math: I owe you $4 for eggs, you owe me $12 for utilities, let's call it even and never speak of it again."

## Transition to Intent Mode

If a post is DIRECTLY asking for a solution ("Is there an app that...", "How do you split...", "Best tool for..."):

THEN switch to intent mode and mention SplitSquad naturally.

Otherwise: stay in soft association. Your bio does the work.
