# Platform: Substack (substack.com)

## Authentication
Already logged in via Chrome profile.

## Navigation
- Open: `https://substack.com/`
- SPA but relatively lightweight
- Content focuses on newsletter articles and discussions

## Finding Relevant Discussions

### Approaches:
- Browse the home feed
- Search: `https://substack.com/search?q=lead+generation`
- Look for notes/posts about SaaS, startup growth, marketing, sales
- Browse specific publications in the SaaS/marketing space
- Notes (short-form posts) are easier to engage with than full articles

### CSS Selectors:
- Notes/posts in feed: look for article cards
- Like button: heart icon
- Comment input: textarea or contenteditable div
- Comment submit: button with "Comment", "Post", or "Reply" text
- Repost/quote: share/repost buttons

### Substack has two interaction modes:
1. **Notes** — short-form content (like Twitter), easy to reply
2. **Articles** — full newsletter posts, comments section at the bottom

## Engaging on a Post

### Steps for Notes:
1. Click into a note or reply directly from feed
2. Find the reply textarea
3. Generate contextual reply
4. Type and submit

### Steps for Articles:
1. Open the article
2. Scroll to the comments section (usually at the bottom)
3. Find the comment input
4. Generate, type, submit
5. Optionally like the article

### Liking:
- Heart icon on notes and articles
- Click before commenting

## Platform-Specific Etiquette

- Substack is writer-focused, conversations are more thoughtful
- Comments on articles should add value to the discussion
- Notes can be more casual and conversational
- Avoid overt pitching in article comments
- Tool mentions work in "what I use" or "what's working" context
- Engaging with notes is less risky for promotion
- Respect the publication's tone and community
